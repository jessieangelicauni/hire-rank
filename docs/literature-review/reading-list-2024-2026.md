# Candidate-ranking reading list (2024–2026)

27 peer-reviewed, open-access journal articles matched to the three stages
of this project's pipeline (see the top-level [README](../../README.md)):
resume screening / agentic HR, semantic candidate-job matching, and
LLM-judge tournament ranking with RAG/fairness evaluation.

**Criteria:** journal articles only (no conference proceedings), published
2024–2026, from IEEE, Springer/Springer Nature, Elsevier, Wiley, Taylor &
Francis, or SAGE, and genuinely open access. arXiv, MDPI, and other
preprint servers were excluded on purpose. Every link below was verified
against a real DOI or publisher page (a few plausible-looking citations
turned up in search results that turned out to be fabricated — those were
dropped rather than included).
