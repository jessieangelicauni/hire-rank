DevOps Engineer
Join our infrastructure team to help build and operate the platform that keeps our services running reliably at scale. You'll work on automation, CI/CD, and observability across a multi-cloud environment.

What you'll need:
- Strong experience with Infrastructure as Code (Terraform ).
- Hands-on experience with Docker and Kubernetes, and GitOps-based delivery (ArgoCD or Flux) in production environments.
- Experience with CI/CD tooling such as GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, or Jenkins.
- Familiarity with observability stacks (Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry, or Datadog).
- Solid understanding of Linux and Networking fundamentals.
- A plus: experience with Helm, service mesh technologies (Istio), and on-call incident response.
