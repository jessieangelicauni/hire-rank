Full-Stack Engineer

Requirements:
- Proficiency in TypeScript across both React or Next.js (frontend) and Node.js (backend, Express or NestJS).
- Experience designing and consuming REST APIs.
- Working knowledge of PostgreSQL and ORMs such as Prisma.
- Comfortable with Git-based workflows and code review.
- Experience with Docker for containerized development and deployment.
- Experience deploying applications to cloud platforms such as AWS or GCP.
- Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions).
- A pragmatic, product-minded approach to engineering trade-offs.
- Nice to have: experience with Stripe payment integrations and Kubernetes.
