Cloud Engineer
We're looking for a Cloud Engineer to design, provision, and operate the cloud infrastructure our products run on. You'll partner with product and platform teams to build environments that are secure, cost-efficient, and ready to scale.

What you'll need:
- Hands-on experience architecting and operating workloads on a major cloud provider (AWS, GCP, or Azure).
- Proficiency with Infrastructure as Code (Terraform or CloudFormation) for provisioning and managing cloud resources.
- Solid understanding of cloud networking (VPC design, load balancing, DNS, and VPN/Direct Connect).
- Experience with containerized workloads on managed Kubernetes (EKS, GKE, or AKS).
- Working knowledge of IAM and cloud security best practices (least-privilege access, encryption, security groups).
- Experience with cost optimization and cloud governance, including budgeting, tagging, and rightsizing resources.
- Familiarity with CI/CD tooling (GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, or Jenkins) for automating infrastructure and deployments.
- Experience with configuration management tools (Ansible, Chef, or Puppet) for fleet configuration and drift management.
- Working knowledge of observability stacks (Prometheus, Grafana, CloudWatch, or Datadog) for monitoring, alerting, and incident response.
