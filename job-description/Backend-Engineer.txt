Backend Engineer
We're hiring a Backend Engineer to help build and scale the services powering our e-commerce platform. You'll work closely with product and frontend teams to design APIs, optimize database performance, and keep our systems reliable as we grow.

Requirements:
- 3+ years of experience building backend services in Node.js and TypeScript or a comparable stack.
- Strong understanding of RESTful API design; experience with GraphQL is a plus.
- Experience with relational databases (PostgreSQL preferred) and query optimization.
- Familiarity with message queues (Kafka) for asynchronous processing.
- Comfortable writing and maintaining unit and integration tests.
- Experience with Docker and Kubernetes for container-based deployments.
- Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions) and Linux environments.
- Nice to have: experience with Redis for caching, JWT-based authentication, and microservices architecture.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience.
