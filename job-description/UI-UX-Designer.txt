UI/UX Designer
We're looking for a UI/UX Designer to shape how users experience our products, from early research through polished, production-ready interfaces. You'll work closely with product managers and engineers to turn ambiguous problems into clear, usable designs.

What you'll need:
- 3+ years of experience designing digital products, with a strong portfolio of shipped work.
- Proficiency in Figma for wireframing, prototyping, and high-fidelity UI design.
- Experience building and maintaining Design Systems and component libraries.
- Solid grounding in User Research and Usability Testing methods to validate design decisions.
- Understanding of Interaction Design and Information Architecture principles.
- Familiarity with Accessibility standards (WCAG) and designing for inclusive use.
- Working knowledge of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to collaborate effectively with engineering on implementation.
- Strong communication skills to present and defend design decisions to stakeholders.
- Advantageous: experience with Motion Design or tools like Adobe XD and Sketch.
